Method

Every number on this board comes from code, and the rules are fixed before the live record starts.

Data and engine

Daily prices and fund distributions come from Yahoo Finance. They are loaded into a Microsoft Qlib data store, and every portfolio is run through Qlib's own backtest engine, with returns and risk measured by Qlib's risk analysis.

Timing and costs

A design decides at a month-end close and trades at the next day's open, paying 5 basis points per trade (10 for TECH-INCOME-30). Holdings drift between rebalances.

Income

Income is the cash the funds themselves paid out, multiplied by the shares held the day before each payment. No shares are sold to create it. "Price only" lines show what the holdings are worth when that cash is taken out instead of reinvested.

Backtest versus live

Everything before 21 Oct 2026 is a hypothetical backtest computed after the fact; the designs were chosen with knowledge of that history. From 21 Oct 2026 the weights are frozen and each design is tracked forward. Each day's published values are also appended to a log that is never rewritten.

TECH-INCOME-30 was chosen using data up to September 2025 and then tested on the following year, which it had not seen. SEMI-ROTATE-10 is a model-driven semiconductor rotation; its history is simulated and its live paper-trading account is private, so it has no live record here.

Updates

After each US market close the data is refreshed, every design is recomputed with the same frozen rules, and the board is republished.
