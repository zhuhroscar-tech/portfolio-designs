How to read

Same chart, every design

  • The design, distributions reinvested
  • Its benchmark
  • Live tracking from here on

Each panel covers the design's own history and starts at 100 on its first day. Log scale, so equal slopes mean equal growth rates.

Income: cash the funds paid out over a year, as a share of the portfolio's value. Since: total return with distributions reinvested, from the design's start. Today: change at the 22 Oct 2026 close.

Research, not investment advice. Results before each design's live date are hypothetical backtests: they were computed after the fact, include no taxes, and assume fills at the next day's open with the stated trading cost. Past performance, real or simulated, does not predict future results. Fund names belong to their issuers; this site is not affiliated with them.
